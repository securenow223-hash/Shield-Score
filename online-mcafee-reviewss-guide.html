What We Review

Office software can look similar from one version to another, but support policies, update models, cloud features and licensing can be very different. Our reviews are designed to explain those differences in plain language.

Office 2016

A traditional desktop Office generation released in 2015. It remains familiar to many users, but its official Microsoft support period has ended.

  • Traditional desktop application model
  • Familiar Word, Excel and PowerPoint experience
  • No ongoing mainstream product support
  • Important compatibility considerations

Microsoft 365

A subscription-based platform that provides Office applications together with cloud-connected services, depending on the plan selected.

  • Regular application updates
  • Cloud-connected features
  • Works across supported devices
  • Different plans for different needs

Microsoft Office 2016 Review

Office 2016 was designed primarily as a traditional desktop productivity suite. It includes applications such as Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Outlook depending on the edition.

Our assessment

Office 2016 can still feel familiar and capable for basic document creation, spreadsheets and presentations. Its interface is straightforward and many users already know how to work with it.

The biggest issue today is not the basic functionality. It is the product's lifecycle. Microsoft lists October 14, 2025 as the end of support date for Office 2016. That means it should not be treated as a currently supported Office release.

Overall suitability today: Best considered for legacy use only

Office 2016 strengths

  • Familiar desktop interface
  • Strong compatibility with traditional Office documents
  • Useful for users who prefer a non-subscription desktop workflow
  • Does not require learning a completely different productivity suite

Office 2016 limitations

  • Official support ended on October 14, 2025.
  • It does not receive the ongoing feature development associated with Microsoft 365.
  • Modern cloud-connected workflows may provide a better experience elsewhere.
  • Organizations should consider lifecycle and security requirements before continuing to rely on it.
Important support status Microsoft officially lists October 14, 2025 as the end of support for Office 2016. Trio Office Review therefore does not recommend presenting Office 2016 as a currently supported product.

Microsoft 365 Review

Microsoft 365 takes a different approach from traditional perpetual Office releases. It combines Office applications with subscription-based services and ongoing updates, depending on the selected plan.

Our assessment

Microsoft 365 is a strong choice for people who want current Office applications, regular updates and cloud-connected productivity features. It is particularly useful for users who work across multiple devices or collaborate with other people.

The main consideration is the subscription model. Users should compare the available plans and determine whether recurring access makes sense for their needs.

Overall suitability: Strong choice for users wanting an actively supported ecosystem

Microsoft 365 strengths

  • Regular application and service updates
  • Cloud-connected productivity features
  • Useful collaboration capabilities
  • Supported across multiple device types, depending on plan and system requirements
  • Access to familiar Office applications

Microsoft 365 limitations

  • Requires a subscription for many consumer and business plans.
  • Features vary according to the plan purchased.
  • Some features require an internet connection or cloud service.
  • Users should review Microsoft's current system requirements before subscribing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Office 2016 still supported?

No. Microsoft's published lifecycle information lists October 14, 2025 as the end of support date for Office 2016.

Is Microsoft 365 the same thing as Office 2016?

No. Office 2016 is a specific older Office release, while Microsoft 365 is a subscription-based service that includes Office applications and other services depending on the plan.

Does Microsoft 365 require an internet connection?

Some Microsoft 365 applications can be used offline after installation, but internet connectivity is important for activation, updates and many cloud-based features. Exact requirements depend on the product and plan.
