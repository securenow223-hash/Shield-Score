Our Editorial View

A security product should be evaluated according to the user's actual needs. Some people may want a broader package containing additional privacy or identity-related features, while others may primarily want antivirus protection.

There is no single security subscription that is automatically the best choice for every user. The appropriate option depends on factors such as device coverage, desired features, operating system compatibility and subscription terms.
